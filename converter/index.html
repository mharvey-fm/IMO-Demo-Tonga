Fouling scale converter

The three scales don’t measure the same thing. NSTM grades what is growing; LoF and IMO grade how much of the area it covers. Give it both and it will express the reading in all three, with the cleaning triggers each one pulls.

How it was derived

    Sources. NSTM Chapter 081 (NAVSEA, rev. 2006) fouling rating scale and cleaning interval criteria; Floerl et al. 2005 Level of Fouling rank scale; IMO resolution MEPC.378(80) table 1, paragraphs 8.8, 9.4, 9.9 and 10.2.

    Read the outputs as ratings, not measurements. The FR is read off the growth description and carries its cover figure separately, so it never converts cleanly to LoF or IMO on its own. LoF and IMO share their breakpoints above 15% cover but diverge below it, where the IMO merges LoF 2 and 3 into a single band. Where a judgement is genuinely ambiguous this tool says so rather than picking a number for you.
